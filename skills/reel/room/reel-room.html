Step 1 · Start

Make your first reel

Three steps. Your clip goes to Claude in the chat, everything else happens on this page.

How to start

  1. Send Claude your clip Paste the file path (or a folder of clips) into your Claude chat, not into this page.
    Make a reel from my clips:
    Paste this into your Claude chat, then add the path after it. No clip yet? Record 20 to 60 s of you talking, phone upright.
    How do I get a file path?
    • Mac: drag the file from Finder into the Claude chat box, the path appears. Or right-click the file, hold Option, and pick "Copy as Pathname".
    • Windows: drag the file into the Claude chat box, or Shift + right-click the file and pick "Copy as path".
    • A whole folder works too: Claude takes the clips inside it.
  2. Pick a style Open the Styles tab and tap the look you want. If you skip it, Claude uses Split deck.
  3. Approve the storyboard Claude shows the plan beat by beat in the Storyboard tab. Approve it and your first cut appears in Dailies.

After that: color, title hook, cover, caption, downloads and posting, each in its own tab. Claude only sees what you do here when you message it, so say "check the Room" in your chat after you pick or approve something.

Optional Beta Learn a style from reels Claude watches a few reels and suggests 3 matching styles.

Most reliable: save 3 to 5 reels as video files and send their paths to Claude in the chat with "learn this style". Handles and links below often fail because Instagram blocks downloads.
